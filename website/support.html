Questions and support

Explore Free and Pro features or try PourSTL in your browser. The iPhone download will be linked on the homepage when available on the App Store.

Get started with PourSTL Web

  1. In My Bar, tap “Show ingredients” on a small screen. Stock your own ingredients or choose “Stock a starter bar.” Use “Can make” to see recipes you have ingredients for.
  2. Choose “New recipe” to save your own recipe. Open any recipe to scale the servings or switch between oz and ml. Use the prep timer to time a shake, stir, or rest.
  3. Choose “Add missing to list” to add ingredients you need. Mark an item purchased to stock it in My Bar.
  4. Open “Bar Assistant” in the header to ask offline questions about what you can make, ingredient stand-ins, or scaled measures.

Web app help

Want a Home Screen icon? Open PourSTL Web in Safari on your iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Your bar did not save? PourSTL Web saves in the browser on this device. Private browsing or blocked storage can limit changes to the current tab.

Copy is blocked? PourSTL Web offers selectable recipe text.

Want to start over? Choose “Clear app data” and confirm. This clears saved data for PourSTL Web from this browser.

Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Open a GitHub issue and include:

  • Whether you were using the website, PourSTL Web, or the iPhone app.
  • Your browser and operating system, or your iPhone model, iOS version, and app build.
  • The steps you took, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot can help.

GitHub issues are public. Remove personal information from screenshots and never include passwords, payment details, or credentials. For a security concern, use the repository’s private vulnerability reporting option when it is available instead of posting vulnerability details publicly.

Using the iPhone app

To scan a barcode, allow camera access when prompted. You can always enter a barcode or ingredient manually in My Bar.

Open “Costing & Batch Prep” inside a recipe to enter package prices, estimate a menu price, set servings, or save a CSV prep sheet. Open “More features” on the recipe tab for Event Planner, or open “Par Levels & Reorder” in My Bar. To restore an existing subscription, open Settings, then PourSTL Pro, and choose “Restore purchases” while connected to the App Store.

The iPhone Bar Assistant answers supported recipe and inventory questions offline. Try “What can I make?” or “Scale Negroni for 4.” It uses local rules, so ask a specific recipe question if it cannot understand a request.

See the privacy page for how PourSTL handles your data.

PourSTL requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
